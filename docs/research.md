# Target evidence

Checked 11 October 2026. Airtable was absent from the rebuild registry. The family is operational databases: custom fields and linked registers, separate from a fixed project board or approval workflow.

[Vendor pricing](https://airtable.com/pricing): Team US$20/user/month and Business US$45/user/month, billed annually. Business therefore models US$27,000 a year for 50 paid users before tax and extras. This is a calculation from a list price, not a verified NZ or AU customer invoice. Enterprise Scale is quoted privately. No local customer adoption was verified, and the buyer-fit score stays low for that reason.

Scores, each out of five: annual bill 3, data-shaped 5, our buyers 2, search demand 4, one-session buildable 5, import 5. Total 24/30. Search-demand score reflects a populated alternative search result page with comparison articles; it is not a measured monthly search volume.

Other unbuilt candidates researched: AssetSonar's current per-asset pricing usually misses the five-figure threshold for a 10-50 person buyer and discovery is central to its value. Canny and UserVoice do not publish current enterprise prices. DealRoom's current pricing page is privately quoted. None supplied a stronger verified price and independent build case during this run.

[Export documentation](https://support.airtable.com/articles/5189551686-getting-started-with-airtable-views) supports the importer. Attachment bytes, formulas, automations and record history do not transfer through CSV. Privacy check sources and scope are in compliance.md. The ten example questions in the README are questions this implementation answers, not evidence that Airtable could never be customised to answer them.
